Research Projects
1. Multi-Variate Time Series Anomaly Detection
Aug 2021 – Present
Exploring three research directions for unsupervised anomaly detection in multivariate time-series from cyber-physical systems:
  • HEIGHTS — Hierarchical Graph Structure Learning modelling subsystem architecture and interactions using two-level multi-view learning. Published in Neurocomputing 2026.
  • FILTER — Nonparametric Feature Clustering that automatically clusters features and uses this for anomaly detection with explainability. Currently under review.
  • ProtoAD — Explainable Anomaly Detection via prototypes representing normal behaviour, improving detection performance and interpretability for end users.
Published Under Review Ongoing
2. Fast Radio Burst (FRB) Identification in Astronomical Time Series
Nov 2022 – Jan 2024
Collaboration with NCRA Pune, NVIDIA, and IIT Palakkad under the NVIDIA AI Technology Center (NVAITC) program. Developed a multi-beam AI classifier to detect FRBs from telescopic time-series data. Contributed to the SPOTLIGHT system deployed at the GMRT, NCRA, Pune.
Deployed · GMRT Pune
3. TCAV — Testing with Concept Activation Vectors
April 2022
Applied TCAV interpretability technique (Google) to identify human-friendly concepts relevant to fruit classification. Built a transfer-learned GoogleNet model on Fruits360 dataset and computed TCAV scores to measure how well each concept was learned. (CS6006: Responsible AI, IIT Palakkad)
4. Image Captioning with CNN-LSTM
April 2022
Built a deep learning framework to automatically generate natural-language captions for images using pretrained CNN embeddings fed into an LSTM. Evaluated using BLEU score and experimented with multiple CNN variants. (CS5007: Deep Learning, IIT Palakkad)
5. Automatic Summarization using Semantic WordClouds
May 2018 – July 2019
MTech thesis. Developed a novel semantic word cloud methodology preserving local word neighbourhoods using K-Medoids clustering on co-occurrence similarity matrices. Evaluated using "Realized Adjacencies" metric. Published at NetCom 2021.
Published · Springer 2021
